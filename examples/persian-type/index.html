Split Persian letters without breaking them.

Animating each letter means giving each its own box. For Arabic-script languages that destroys joining: letters fall back to isolated forms. Veyra computes Unicode joining types and adds zero-width joiners on the connecting sides, keeps lam-alef ligatures whole, and isolates opposite-direction runs.
